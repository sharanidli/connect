How to play

Each day brings two puzzles. In each one, three clues point to the same thing. Name it.

  1. Read clue 1 and type your guess. Spelling needn't be perfect.
  2. Wrong guess? The next clue opens and you guess again. Not sure? Tap the next clue to open it.
  3. Fewer clues, more points: 15, 10 or 5.
  4. Still stuck after clue 3? Pick from four choices for 2 points.

New puzzles every day at midnight.
