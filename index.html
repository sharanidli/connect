How to play

Each day brings two puzzles. In each one, three clues point to the same thing. Name it.

  1. Read clue 1 and type your guess.
  2. Not sure? Tap clue 2 for another hint, then clue 3. A wrong guess also opens the next clue.
  3. Fewer clues, more points: 15, 10 or 5.
  4. Still stuck after clue 3? Pick from four choices for 2 points.

Spelling doesn't have to be perfect. New puzzles every day at midnight.

This pilot records anonymous play data (no names or contact details) to see how the puzzles play.
